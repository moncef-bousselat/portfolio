Team project pulling Google Earth Engine satellite imagery into Unity to
rebuild real landscapes in 3D — elevation, wind and vegetation data — then
simulating how a wildfire spreads from a chosen ignition point.

Using real terrain rather than synthetic test surfaces is what made the output
worth anything: the same semi-empirical model behaves very differently on an
actual slope with actual fuel distribution.

The work ended up published on ResearchGate.
