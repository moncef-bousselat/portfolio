Built with a team at the Google DevFest hackathon. It follows a computer user's
movements and facial features to estimate whether they are focused, and records
that over time.

It works. It also makes the surveillance problem obvious the moment you imagine
it deployed anywhere real — which was, in the end, the more interesting outcome
than the model.
